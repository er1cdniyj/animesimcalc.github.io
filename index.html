Boss Fight Strategies

How to Beat ANY boss

Bosses in Anime Simulator are tough, especially for low levels. Here's how to defeat them:

  • Tailed Form + Gold Digger (before entering the boss)
  • Tsunami (7 seconds before boss)
  • Meteorite Destruction (3 seconds before boss)
  • Gold Digger C Move (when boss spawns)
  • Double jump
  • Extinction Rain (hold) + Divine Blessing + Multi-Bomb Shuriken + other random powers
Pro Tip: A good companion to use is Freeah, as it provides healing and is effective against bosses.

Beatin Dungeons

Dungeons are similar to bosses:

  • Mostly the same moves as for bosses.
  • Sound Breathing is another meta transformation:
    • Good damage
    • Low cooldowns
  • Some players still prefer Gold Digger.
  • With the moves above, you can grind dungeons efficiently. Have a good Companion and use Shadows.

General Gameplay Tips

Efficient Stat Training

Maximize your training efficiency:

  • Focus on one stat at a time
  • Upgrade your multiplier for better gains

Coin Farming Strategies

Earn coins faster:

  • Complete daily quests
  • Farm in areas matching your power level
  • Use coin multipliers when available
